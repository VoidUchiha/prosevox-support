ProseVox Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

ProseVox ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a privacy-first web novel reader and assistive text-to-speech utility for Apple iOS. We believe your reading habits, bookmarks, and preferences are entirely private to you.

We do not track, collect, monetize, or sell your personal data. The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking frameworks, or behavioral analytics brokers.

2. Data Handled by ProseVox

On-Device Local Data:

  • Reading History & Progress: Recent novel chapters and scroll percentages are saved strictly locally on your Apple device using secure iOS storage APIs.
  • Passage Bookmarks: Paragraph snippets and timestamps you bookmark are stored solely on your device.
  • Display & Audio Preferences: Font size, theme choices (Dark, Midnight, Light), and speech synthesizer settings (rate, pitch) are saved in your device's local application sandbox.

Optional Authentication:

ProseVox offers optional sign-in via Google OAuth solely to allow users to sync novel library folders across devices with the web service. Authentication tokens and session cookies are transmitted directly through secure HTTPS connections. ProseVox never receives, logs, or stores your Google password.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

  • No Advertising SDKs: ProseVox contains zero advertising networks.
  • No Tracking Across Apps: We do not access or collect the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • No Location Data: ProseVox never requests or tracks your geographic location.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

Under Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), you have complete control over your data and the immediate right to delete it:

  • Instant On-Device Deletion: In the App, navigate to Account & Settings → Reading Data & Storage and tap Clear Reading History or Clear All Bookmarks. This instantly erases local records.
  • Account Deletion: If signed in, navigate to Account & Settings → Account & Data Management to delete your account and associated synced library records.
  • Email Request: You can contact us directly at prosevoxsupport@gmail.com with the subject Privacy Data Deletion Request. All requests are processed within 48 hours.

5. Children's Privacy

ProseVox does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
