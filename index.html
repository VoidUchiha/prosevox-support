Account & Data Deletion (App Store Guideline 5.1.1)

We respect your privacy and provide complete control over your data. In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), users can delete their data or account anytime:

  • Local Reading Data: In the ProseVox app, open Account & Settings → Reading Data & Storage and tap Clear Reading History or Clear All Bookmarks. This immediately deletes local databases on your device.
  • Account Deletion: If you are signed in, navigate to Account & Settings → Account & Data Management to manage or delete your linked credentials.
  • Email Data Deletion Request: You can also email prosevoxsupport@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, and our team will erase all associated records within 48 hours.

Content Moderation & Copyright (DMCA)

ProseVox is a client-side reader and text-to-speech utility. We take intellectual property and safety seriously:

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Text-to-Speech (TTS) Narration work?
While reading any chapter, tap the Play button at the bottom of the screen to start paragraph-by-paragraph speech playback using native Apple iOS speech synthesis. You can adjust the playback speed, pitch, and voice in Reading Settings. Narration continues seamlessly when the screen is locked.
How do I bookmark a specific paragraph or passage?
Tap on any paragraph while reading, or tap the bookmark icon in the top navigation toolbar. Saved passages can be viewed at any time under the Passages tab in your Library.
Is VoiceOver supported?
Yes, ProseVox is fully VoiceOver enabled with labeled controls, accessibility traits, slider values, and synchronized spoken paragraph focus.
Do I need an account to read novels?
No. You can search, browse, read, and listen to novels without signing in. Signing in is only optional if you wish to sync novel library folders across devices.
